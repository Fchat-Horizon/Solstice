Built on Horizon 2.4.0.

Solstice can now pull your chat log history off Horizon on your computer, exporting logs works on both platforms, and a large import no longer freezes the app.

## Changes since v2026.6.4

- **Log syncing with Horizon.** Pair Solstice with Horizon on your computer to copy your chat log history onto your phone over your local network. In Horizon, open "Manage Data" and choose "Sync with Solstice"; in Solstice, scan the pairing QR code. Transfers run directly between the two devices, and your existing logs are merged rather than overwritten. The computer needs Horizon 2.4.0 or later.
- **Syncing copes with big log folders.** Logs transfer one bounded batch at a time in both directions instead of being packed into a single archive, so neither device holds the whole log set in memory and folders that were simply too big to sync now go through. The sync screen shows progress as each batch lands, and a conversation whose stored log turns out to be damaged is reported at the end instead of quietly ending the transfer.
- **Log exports go through the app rather than the browser.** Exporting from the log viewer leaned on a browser download that the WebView ignores, so it did nothing at all on iOS and only partly worked on Android, with no error either way. The export now hands the file to the app itself, which drops it into Downloads on Android and offers the share sheet on iOS.
- **Saves reach Downloads on a fresh phone.** Writing into Downloads failed silently on a device where that folder did not exist yet. It is created first now, which covers backup exports, log exports and the diagnostic log.
- **A large import no longer freezes the app.** Importing a desktop backup locked everything up until it finished: 16 seconds for a 58 MB backup, 85 seconds for a 296 MB one. The import now runs in small batches and gives the app room to redraw between them, so it stays responsive throughout.
- **A failed import can be handed over.** An import that fails now writes a trace of what it was doing and offers to save it, so you can report more than the word "failed". It records entries by shape, never your character names or any chat content. An import killed outright by the system leaves no error behind at all, so that trace is folded into the diagnostic log on the next launch instead.
- **Manage Data survives signing out (#17).** Its tabs no longer freeze and its dialogs open again after logging out and back in.
- **Tidier sign-in screen.** The checkboxes and loading spinner match the rest of the app, the stray theme name under the picker is gone, and the screen now renders in whichever theme you picked rather than only the built-in ones.
- **iOS builds against the current SDK (#20).** Rebuilt for the iOS 26 and Xcode 27 SDK with scene-based lifecycle support. The minimum supported version is now iOS 15.
- **Two iOS install channels.** The public SideStore source always points at the newest published release, and the rolling test channel identifies itself separately as "Solstice (Test)", so you can subscribe to either or both.

## From Horizon 2.4.0

- **Dates and times follow your language.** Timestamps use your display language's formatting instead of always using English.
- **Translated summaries and correct plurals.** The messages shown after an export or import are translated, and counted things ("5 messages", "1 channel") use the right plural form for your language, including the missing Spanish plural in the export summary.
- **Character pickers show avatars.** The character select dropdowns, such as on the profile viewer and the friends dialog, show the selected character's avatar.
- **Friend requests show as pending.** Sending a request from the profile viewer no longer lists you as that person's friend before they have accepted.
- **Channels stay grouped.** The grace period for pruning grouped channels is more sensible, so channels you joined correctly are no longer ungrouped moments after connecting.
- **Ads and smart filters behave.** Smart filters work with "Colorize ads" off, a busy ad queue no longer lets ads slip past unfiltered, and changing a filter setting now applies to ads already on screen.
- **The quick switcher respects your channel groups.** It follows your group order again.
- **HQ profile pictures toggle without a restart.** Turning the setting on or off takes effect immediately.
- **"Standardize profile fonts" no longer breaks BBCode.** Profiles using characters that parse into brackets render correctly, and emoji stay visible.
- **Guestbook counts are accurate.** Guestbooks with more than 15 posts show the real number.
- **Searching the log viewer scrolls properly.** Scrolling up while searching loads older messages without having to scroll again.
- **Friend colors in the All Friends tab.** Names are no longer all green when you color friends and bookmarks differently.
- **Accessibility fixes.** Colorblind mode colors and button text contrast were corrected, and screen reader labels no longer claim every public and private channel is official.
- **Species matching.** Lamia and naga are no longer scored as the same species when matching profiles.
